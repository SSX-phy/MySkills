# Nature / Nature Portfolio — Cover Letter Reference

Source: https://support.nature.com/en/support/solutions/articles/6000245674-cover-letter

Applies to: Nature, Nature Communications, Nature Methods, Nature Physics, Nature Chemistry,
Nature Biotechnology, and other Nature Portfolio journals.

## Mapping
- nature, nature communications, nature methods, nature physics, nature chemistry, nature biotechnology → `nature`
---

## Status

Cover letter is **optional** but strongly encouraged. Treat it as a mandatory for quality submissions.

---

## Structure

Use the standard 4-paragraph formalism. No deviations required.

---

## Paragraph 4 — Mandatory Verbatim Statement

Include the following sentence verbatim in ¶4:

> "We confirm that this manuscript has not been published elsewhere and is not under consideration
> by another journal. All authors have approved the manuscript and agree with its submission
> to [journal name]."

Replace `[journal name]` with the exact journal title.

---

## After Paragraph 4 — Optional Reviewer Suggestions

After completing the four paragraphs, offer the user:

> *"Do you want to suggest reviewers? Nature encourages 3–5 names with affiliations and a brief
> note on their expertise. If so, provide the names and I'll add them."*

Format if provided:
> We suggest the following reviewers:
> - [Name], [Institution] — [brief note on expertise/relevance]

Also offer space to note any **excluded reviewers** (those with a conflict of interest):
> We request that the following individuals not be asked to review this manuscript: [Name, reason].

---

## Optional Additions (include only if applicable)

- **COI**: If the user confirms a conflict, add a sentence in ¶4.
- **Related submissions**: If the user has related manuscripts under review elsewhere, mention in ¶4:
  > "A related manuscript describing [brief description] has been submitted to [journal]."
- **Data/code availability**: If the user wants to note it, add one sentence in ¶4.
- **Prior editor correspondence**: If the user had a pre-submission inquiry, reference it in ¶4.

---

## Do's and Don'ts

**Do:**
- Keep the letter concise — editors read hundreds; longer is not more impressive.
- Make ¶3 specific to the Nature title (not just "Nature portfolio").

**Don't:**
- Restate the abstract.
- Start ¶1 with a method or with "We are pleased to submit".
